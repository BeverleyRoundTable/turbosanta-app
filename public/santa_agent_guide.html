6 Conversational Timeouts & Privacy (Advanced Settings)

Click on Settings → Advanced in your agent dashboard and apply these exact timings to prevent premature disconnects and safeguard child privacy:

Setting Value Explanation
Turn model Turn V3 State-of-the-art turn taking detector
Take turn after silence 7 Seconds Gives children ample time to formulate thoughts
End conversation after silence 15 Seconds Closes inactive calls naturally after 15s quiet
Max conversation duration 180 Seconds 3-minute limit before Santa dashes back to workshop
Speculative turn ON Reduces latency while child speaks
Client events audio, interruption, user_transcript, agent_response Powers real-time chat bubbles and animations
Store Call Audio OFF Critical for safeguarding — no child voice audio is stored
Retention Period 30 Days Text transcripts auto-delete after 30 days
7 Security Allowlist (Host Protection)

Click on Settings → Security. Under Allowlist, add each of the following hostnames so your widget can connect securely:

roundtablesantasleigh.co.uk [town].roundtablesantasleigh.co.uk turbosanta-app.pages.dev
  • Toggle Fail when Origin header is missing: ON.
  • Under Overrides, toggle Text only: ON (leave all others OFF).
8 Copy Your Agent ID into TurboSanta

Look at the top of your agent page or URL in ElevenLabs to find your Agent ID (e.g. agent_2101m5d5sktze0rb4mchkhaz1aqj).

  1. Copy your Agent ID.
  2. Open your TurboSanta Admin Portal → Feature Slots.
  3. Select Santa AI Voice Call (ElevenLabs Voice Hotline) for Slot 1 or Slot 2.
  4. Paste your Agent ID into the ElevenLabs Agent ID for Santa Voice Call box.
  5. Click Save Sleigh Settings.
