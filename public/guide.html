Welcome to TurboSanta

TurboSanta is the UK & Ireland Round Table Santa Sleigh tracking, spatial address lookup, and fundraising platform. It gives every Table an instant, lightning-fast, high-availability Santa Sleigh tracking experience powered by Cloudflare Pages, Workers, and a multi-tenant D1 SQL Database.

Universal Multi-Tenant

One Repo, Every Table

Every Round Table uses the same unified high-speed system. Simply supply your table slug (e.g. ?table=beverley, ?table=ellon, ?table=doncaster) and the platform automatically loads your branding, route nights, street timetable, and donations.

D1 SQL Database

No More Slow Sheets

Routes, live telemetry, volunteer rosters, and donations are stored directly in Cloudflare D1 with global edge caching. Real-time updates deliver in under 50 milliseconds with zero rate limiting.

One-Click Upgrade

1.0 to 2.0 Fast Transfer (Google Sheets & Excel)

Migrating a Round Table from TurboSanta 1.0 to 2.0 takes under 10 seconds. Tables do not need to re-type routes or recreate volunteer rosters.

How It Works

  1. Open your 1.0 Google Sheet in your web browser.
  2. Click File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx).
  3. Open the TurboSanta Importer (or open Admin Portal → 1.0 Excel / Sheets Importer).
  4. Drag and drop your .xlsx file. The parser runs client-side in your browser in milliseconds.
  5. Review the live preview showing extracted routes, street names, volunteer roster, and branding settings.
  6. Click Auto-Populate TurboSanta 2.0 Database. Your Cloudflare D1 SQL database is updated immediately!

What Data Gets Transferred?

🗺️ Routes & Streets

Every nightly route name, date, start time, end time, and GPX track link. The comma-separated street list is automatically split and sequenced into individual route_streets database records to power the Address Lookup tool.

👥 Volunteers & Roster

Full supporter contact list (Name, Preferred Route, Role, Mobile Phone, Email, Organization, Bucket Number) is imported directly into the SQL volunteers table.

🛷 Sleigh Branding & Links

Sleigh display name, charity name, Zeffy/Stripe donation links, fundraising target (£), PA announcement presets, and volunteer roles.

🤝 Sponsors & Donations

Corporate route sponsors, logos, pledged amounts, and historical bucket donation totals.

Route Planner & GPX Direct Upload

TurboSanta features a complete route planning engine that accepts direct .gpx file uploads while preserving dense road geometry and planned stops.

1. Direct GPX Upload in Portal

In the Admin Portal (Route Management):

  • Open Add New Route or Edit Route.
  • Tag the Route Name (e.g. East Route).
  • Drag & drop your .gpx file directly into the modal.
  • Dense trackpoints (<trkpt>) and planned stops (<wpt>) are parsed automatically.

2. Visual Route Planner Tool

Use the built-in Route Planner (/route_planner.html):

  • Click anywhere on roads to snap waypoints using OpenStreetMap.
  • Mark specific points as Planned Stops (Grottos/Pubs).
  • Click Detect Streets to auto-extract every visited street via reverse-geocoding.
  • Export high-density (5m/10m) GPX files.

Volunteer Tracking & Crew Hub (crew.html)

Every volunteer application is saved directly into your Table's Cloudflare D1 SQL database. Crew leaders have a complete operational dashboard on the night.

Volunteer Sign-Up

Supporters visit /crew.html?table=[slug] to register their name, mobile phone number, preferred route night, and role (Bucket Collector, Safety Walker, Driver).

Route Night Check-In & Safety

Crew leaders log in with the table admin secret to:

  • Check in arriving volunteers.
  • Assign collection bucket numbers (e.g. Bucket #4).
  • Complete pre-departure vehicle & hitch safety checklists.
  • Close route and log total cash collected.

God Mode Admin Dashboard

God Mode (/god_mode.html?table=[slug]) is your mission control center on route night.

  • Live PA System — push instant announcements to the live tracker (e.g. "Santa is at the Market Cross for 5 minutes!").
  • Crew Alert — broadcast tactical notes to the volunteers' Crew Hub without notifying the public.
  • Telemetry Health — monitor sleigh speed, road name, battery, and GPS fix rate.
  • Gift Aid Claims — review and export HMRC-compliant Gift Aid schedules in 1 click.

Backend Architecture & REST API

TurboSanta runs on Cloudflare Workers + D1 Database. All endpoints support ?table=[slug] multi-tenancy:

GET https://turbosanta-api.beverley247.workers.dev/api/payload?table=beverley
Returns: Table settings, scheduled routes, streets list, and latest sleigh GPS position.
GET https://turbosanta-api.beverley247.workers.dev/api/live-gps?table=beverley
Returns: High-speed live coordinates (lat, lng, speed, road_name, timestamp).
PUT https://turbosanta-api.beverley247.workers.dev/api/telemetry
Payload: { table_id, route_id, lat, lng, speed, road_name } — GPS beacon ingest.
POST https://turbosanta-api.beverley247.workers.dev/api/volunteers?table=beverley
Payload: { routeName, name, role, phone, email, organisation } — Saves volunteer registration into SQL.
