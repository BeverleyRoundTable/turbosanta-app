Welcome to TurboSanta

TurboSanta is the UK & Ireland Round Table Santa Sleigh tracking, spatial address lookup, and fundraising platform. It gives every Table an instant, lightning-fast, high-availability Santa Sleigh tracking experience powered by Cloudflare Pages, Workers, and a multi-tenant D1 SQL Database.

Universal Multi-Tenant

One Repo, Every Table

Every Round Table operates on its own dedicated town subdomain: https://beverley.roundtablesantasleigh.co.uk (e.g. beverley., ringwood., shirley.) and the platform automatically loads your branding, route nights, street timetable, and donations.

D1 SQL Database

No More Slow Sheets

Routes, live telemetry, doorstep email pins, volunteer rosters, Gift Aid declarations, and donations are stored directly in Cloudflare D1 with global edge caching. Real-time updates deliver in under 50 milliseconds with zero rate limiting.

One-Click Upgrade

1.0 to 2.0 Fast Transfer (Google Sheets & Excel)

Migrating a Round Table from TurboSanta 1.0 to 2.0 takes under 10 seconds. Tables do not need to re-type routes or recreate volunteer rosters.

How It Works

  1. Open your 1.0 Google Sheet in your web browser.
  2. Click File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx).
  3. Open the TurboSanta Importer (or open Admin Portal → 1.0 Excel / Sheets Importer).
  4. Drag and drop your .xlsx file. The parser runs client-side in your browser in milliseconds.
  5. Review the live preview showing extracted routes, street names, volunteer roster, and branding settings.
  6. Click Auto-Populate TurboSanta 2.0 Database. Your Cloudflare D1 SQL database is updated immediately!

What Data Gets Transferred?

🗺️ Routes & Streets

Every nightly route name, date, start time, end time, and GPX track link. The comma-separated street list is automatically split and sequenced into individual route_streets database records to power the Address Lookup tool.

👥 Volunteers & Roster

Full supporter contact list (Name, Preferred Route, Role, Mobile Phone, Email, Organization, Bucket Number) is imported directly into the SQL volunteers table.

🛷 Sleigh Branding & Links

Sleigh display name, charity name, Zeffy/Stripe/JustGiving donation links, fundraising target (£), PA announcement presets, and volunteer roles.

🤝 Sponsors & Donations

Corporate route sponsors, logos, pledged amounts, and historical bucket donation totals.

Route Planner & GPX Direct Upload

TurboSanta features a complete route planning engine that accepts direct .gpx file uploads while preserving dense road geometry, planned Santa Stops, and multi-sponsor route cards.

1. Direct GPX Upload in Portal

In the Admin Portal (Route Management):

  • Open Add New Route or Edit Route.
  • Tag the Route Name (e.g. East Route) and add one or more Route Sponsors.
  • Drag & drop your .gpx file directly into the modal.
  • Dense trackpoints (<trkpt>) and planned stops (<wpt>) are parsed automatically.

2. Visual Route Planner Tool

Use the built-in Route Planner (https://beverley.roundtablesantasleigh.co.uk/route_planner.html):

  • Click anywhere on roads to snap waypoints using OpenStreetMap.
  • Mark specific points as Planned Stops (Grottos/Pubs).
  • Click Detect Streets to auto-extract every visited street via reverse-geocoding.
  • Export high-density (5m/10m) GPX files or save directly to your Table's D1 database.

Automated Resident Comms

Doorstep Pin Alerts, "Tonight's the Night!" & Weather Rain Checks

TurboSanta connects the Address & Route Schedule map (/address) directly with the Live Tracker (/tracker) and automated Cloudflare Worker email alerts so families never miss the sleigh or wait out in pouring rain.

📍 1. Pre-Season Pin Drop & Proximity Alert

All uploaded GPX routes are visible on https://beverley.roundtablesantasleigh.co.uk/address before route night. When a resident searches their postcode or taps their house on the map, they can subscribe for a free Doorstep Email Alert.

  • Their pin is saved in D1 (subscribers) and synced via localStorage + postMessage to the Live Tracker to display their personal street ETA countdown.
  • On route night, as soon as the live sleigh GPS enters your configured proximity radius (default 400m in Admin Portal → Settings), the worker automatically emails them that Santa is minutes away!

🔔 2. "Tonight's the Night!" Departure Email

Wakes up everyone subscribed on tonight's route when the sleigh leaves the depot so families can get coats and boots ready and open the live tracker.

  • Automatic: Fires automatically the first time the driver starts broadcasting live GPS on tonight's route.
  • Manual 1-Click: Can also be triggered anytime from Admin Portal → Routes → 🔔 Tonight's Email button on the route card.
  • Includes the route start/end window, their saved pin label, live tracker link (https://beverley.roundtablesantasleigh.co.uk/tracker), and online donate button.

3. 1-Click Weather Postponement ("Rain Check") Broadcast

High winds or torrential rain forcing a postponement? In Admin Portal → Routes, click the amber 🌧️ Rain Check button on any route card:

  • Enter a short weather explanation and an optional Rescheduled Date.
  • The route is immediately marked with a 🌧️ WEATHER UPDATE — POSTPONED badge and note across your public website, and your live announcement banner is updated automatically.
  • Every subscriber pinned on that route is immediately emailed with the postponement notice and new date so no children are left waiting outside in bad weather.

JustGiving Live Auto-Sync

Paste your public JustGiving Campaign or Fundraising Page URL in Admin Portal → Fundraising → JustGiving & Ticket Tailor Live Auto-Sync and click Save & Sync Now.

The worker automatically reads your live JustGiving total and adds only the delta into your thermometer so it safely coexists with cash buckets and Ticket Tailor!

REST API + Real-Time Webhook

Ticket Tailor Integration

Works in two ways (individually or together):

  • API Auto-Sync: Enter your Ticket Tailor API Key (sk_...) and optional Event ID (ev_...) to pull completed order revenue automatically.
  • Instant Webhook: Add https://turbosanta-api.beverley247.workers.dev/api/webhooks/tickettailor?table=beverley in Ticket Tailor → Settings → Webhooks (order.created / order.completed).

Zeffy (100% Free)

Captures zero-fee online donations. Automatically converts pence to pounds and logs donor names into D1.

Stripe & Square

Listens for Stripe checkout.session.completed and Square payment.updated contactless reader events.

SumUp (Street Card Taps)

Connects mobile card readers used by street bucket collectors. Street contactless taps immediately feed the live thermometer!

PayPal

Captures completed PayPal payments via PAYMENT.CAPTURE.COMPLETED webhooks.

Cash Bucket Tally

Log physical coin/note counts via the Admin Portal's dual-counter Cash Bucket modal or directly from the Driver Beacon at the end of a route.

Custom / Zapier

Accepts standard JSON {"amount": 10.00, "donorName": "Jane"} from any third-party integration.

+25% HMRC Uplift & Community Impact

Pre-Checkout +25% Gift Aid & Local Cause Grant Nominations

Maximise every pound raised and show residents where their donations stay locally:

🎁 Pre-Checkout +25% HMRC Gift Aid Flow

When Enable Gift Aid is turned on in Admin Portal → Settings, clicking Donate Now on your public site (or scanning a bucket QR code with https://beverley.roundtablesantasleigh.co.uk/?donate=1) presents a fast 1-step UK taxpayer declaration modal before opening your JustGiving / Ticket Tailor / Zeffy checkout.

  • Saves HMRC-required fields (First Name, Surname, House No. & Street, Postcode) into D1 (gift_aid).
  • Includes a 1-click "Skip Gift Aid — Go Straight to Online Donation" link so checkout is never blocked.
  • Export all declarations as an HMRC R68 CSV in 1 click from God Mode or Admin Portal.

Sleigh Driver Cockpit, 3D Sat-Nav & End-of-Night Cash Logger

The Driver Cockpit provides sleigh navigators and drivers with two operational modes tailored for route night:

1. 3D Sat-Nav & Voice Navigation

Accessible via Admin Portal → Driver Cockpit Mode or directly at https://beverley.roundtablesantasleigh.co.uk/tracker?driver=1.

  • Heading-Up 3D Map: 60° pitched 3D map that smoothly rotates to face your direction of travel like Google Maps or Waze.
  • Turn-by-Turn Voice Directions: Speaks maneuvers aloud (e.g. "In 100 meters, turn left onto Lairgate") using the Web Speech API.
  • Road Snapping: Snaps GPS to pre-planned GPX street lines with turn countdowns.
  • Auto Telemetry & Offline Queue: Sends live GPS fixes every 4s to Cloudflare D1 with IndexedDB buffer if mobile signal drops.

2. Battery-Saver Beacon & Cash Bucket Logger

Designed for long nights or low phone battery at https://beverley.roundtablesantasleigh.co.uk/beacon.html.

  • Ultra-Low Power: Screen-off background transmission with WakeLock & Audio Keep-Alive.
  • Automatic "Tonight's the Night!" Trigger: Starting the beacon on tonight's route automatically emails subscribed families on that route.
  • End-of-Night Cash Bucket Quick-Log: When stopping the beacon at the end of the route (or tapping 🪣 £ CASH in the top bar), a 1-tap modal lets the driver log tonight's cash bucket count directly into the live thermometer (POST /api/donations/cash).

God Mode Admin Dashboard

God Mode (https://beverley.roundtablesantasleigh.co.uk/god_mode.html) is your mission control center on route night.

  • Live PA System — push instant announcements to the live tracker (e.g. "Santa is at the Market Cross for 5 minutes!").
  • Crew Alert — broadcast tactical notes to the volunteers' Crew Hub without notifying the public.
  • Telemetry Health — monitor sleigh speed, road name, battery, and GPS fix rate.
  • Gift Aid Claims — review and export HMRC-compliant Gift Aid schedules in 1 click.

Artificial Intelligence & Operations Suite (Gemini 3.8 Flash)

TurboSanta includes an integrated suite of purpose-built artificial intelligence tools powered by Google Gemini 3.8 Flash. The AI engine is designed to save volunteer organisers hours of manual work during freezing December nights, while keeping community safety and child protection front and centre.

Multi-Model Fallback Cascade & Model Selector

In Admin Portal → Settings → AI Model, each Table can select their preferred Gemini model (defaulting to gemini-3.8-flash). All AI operations run through TurboSanta's Cascade Engine:

gemini-3.8-flash (Primary) → gemini-3.7-flash → gemini-3.6-flash → gemini-2.5-flash → gemini-1.5-flash (Emergency Backup)

If Google ever experiences temporary regional rate limits or cloud hiccups on the primary model, requests seamlessly cascade down without failure.

🎙️ 1. Magic Mailbox: Voice Transcription & Wish Extractor

Children record audio messages directly into their phone or tablet microphone. Gemini listens to the audio to:

  • Transcribe what the child said into text
  • Extract top Christmas wishes and requested gifts
  • Screen for safeguarding or emotional distress concerns
  • Draft an authentic, personalized Santa script for elf volunteers to copy or read aloud

📱 2. One-Click Social Media & PR Wrap-Up

At the end of a 3-hour tour, click "AI Social Post" in Cockpit or Route Manager to pull tonight's live statistics:

  • Facebook & Instagram: Community-first copy celebrating miles walked, cash raised, and sponsor shout-outs
  • WhatsApp Volunteer Crew: Quick thank-you message to bucket collectors
  • Local Press Release: Formatted release ready to email local news desks

🛡️ 3. Public Spotting Notes & Cheer Wall Auto-Moderation

Supporters post live cheer notes and Santa sightings from the tracker:

  • Real-time <200ms evaluation filters profanity, harassment, or accidental home addresses
  • Instantly approves safe, joyful messages so they appear live on the tracker ticker
  • Admins retain full override controls (Approve, Reject, Delete) in Tab 4

📸 4. Digital Memory Book: Safety Scanner & Santa Polaroid Captions

When families upload polaroid photos meeting Santa on the street:

  • Gemini Vision automatically verifies child safeguarding and family-friendliness
  • Generates a charming 1-sentence photo caption signed by Santa Claus referencing knitted hats, reindeer antlers, or smiles

🦺 5. Automated Volunteer Marshaling & Tactical Briefing Generator

Generates tailored WhatsApp and clipboard safety briefings for volunteer elves before departure, inspecting muster points, street types, cul-de-sacs, main road crossings, and wet weather gear protocols.

Billing Safety & Abuse Protection

TurboSanta is engineered with enterprise-grade abuse prevention so your Gemini API account is safe from unexpected usage spikes:

  • Per-IP Rate Limiting: Every public endpoint (voice recordings, cheer notes, polaroids, letters to Santa) is throttled by Cloudflare Workers (e.g. 5 requests per 5 minutes per IP).
  • Hard Monthly Caps: Google Cloud Billing supports hard monthly caps (e.g., £10/month) with proactive email alerts at 50%, 90%, and 100%.
  • Micro-Cost Token Architecture: Spotting note filters use ~10 tokens, and photo/audio tasks run asynchronously in the background via Cloudflare Workers' ctx.waitUntil, keeping total seasonal operating costs under £1.00 for a typical Table.

Data Protection & Privacy Architecture (GDPR)

TurboSanta implements strict architectural boundaries between publicly accessible spectator data and protected volunteer / financial data.

Public Data (Spectators)

  • Live Sleigh GPS coordinates, speed, and heading (/api/live-gps)
  • Route schedules, start times, postponed notes, and street listings (/api/payload)
  • Spatial address lookup & arrival estimations
  • Aggregated total raised (£), fundraising target, and approved community grants
  • Route sponsors, headline sponsors, and community partners
  • Volunteer sign-up & grant nomination submissions (users cannot see others' entries)

Protected Data (Admin Only)

  • Volunteer Personal Info: Names, mobile numbers, emails, bucket IDs, and check-in status (requires Admin Secret; 401 Unauthorized without auth).
  • HMRC Gift Aid Declarations: Full taxpayer names, house numbers, and postcodes (accessible only via authenticated /api/gift-aid/export).
  • Webhook Secrets, Ticket Tailor Keys & Passwords: Stored securely in Cloudflare D1 and masked in public payloads.
  • Subscriber Doorstep Emails: Stored privately in D1 and used strictly for route departure, proximity, and weather postponement alerts.

Backend Architecture & REST API

TurboSanta runs on Cloudflare Workers + D1 Database. All endpoints support ?table=[slug] multi-tenancy:

GET https://turbosanta-api.beverley247.workers.dev/api/payload?table=beverley
Returns: Table settings, scheduled routes, streets list, approved community grants, and latest sleigh GPS position.
POST https://turbosanta-api.beverley247.workers.dev/api/routes/notify-departure • POST /api/routes/postpone
Sends "Tonight's the Night!" departure emails or Weather Rain Check postponement emails to route subscribers.
POST https://turbosanta-api.beverley247.workers.dev/api/donations/sync-external • POST /api/webhooks/tickettailor
